# Sah-AI — 90-Second Demo Script

> Target audience: Google Hackathon judges
> Key message: AI-powered recovery support that prioritizes safety at every layer

---

## Opening (10s)

> "Sah-AI is a voice-first recovery support companion. It's designed for one scenario:
> 3am, someone in recovery is struggling, and they need help — but they can't type."

## The Home Screen (10s)

*(Show the breathing amber button)*

> "This pulsing button is the entire interface. It's a visual breathing exercise
> AND the entry point. No login, no forms, no typing. Just tap and talk."

## Voice Companion Demo (20s)

*(Tap the button, show it connecting)*

> "The browser connects directly to Gemini's Live API — no server proxy, sub-200ms latency.
> The voice uses DBT distress-tolerance techniques: grounding exercises, urge surfing, breathing guidance."

*(Show a brief exchange if time permits)*

> "Behind the scenes, every turn is classified in parallel. If a crisis is detected..."

## Crisis Bypass (15s)

*(Show the crisis card appearing with 988 and 911 links)*

> "...the AI session immediately terminates and the user sees pre-authored crisis content.
> These are real `tel:` and `sms:` links — one tap to 988 or 911.
> This content is NEVER generated by the AI. It's clinician-reviewable, static, and always correct."

## Emergency Triage (10s)

*(Navigate to /triage, show the 4-option decision tree)*

> "For users who need structured guidance, the triage flow asks one question:
> 'What's happening right now?' Each path leads to vetted resources with large tap targets."

## Script Generator (15s)

*(Navigate to /scripts, tap a preset like "Craving after conflict")*

> "The script generator creates personalized support scripts — for the person in recovery
> OR their caregiver. Every script passes through a deterministic safety filter
> before reaching the user. If the filter rejects it, a pre-authored fallback is served instead."

*(Show the AI-generated label and the safety filter badge)*

## Technical Differentiation (10s)

> "Three architectural choices make this different:
> 1. **Fail-closed classification** — any error defaults to the safest path
> 2. **Static crisis content** — the AI selects, but never writes, crisis guidance
> 3. **Deterministic safety filter** — regex-based, zero latency, fully testable
>
> 23 unit tests cover the safety pipeline. Zero generated text reaches the user unfiltered."

## Close (10s)

> "Sah-AI is a wellness tool built on Gemini. It's not a replacement for 988 or clinical care —
> and it's designed to never pretend to be one. That's the point."
